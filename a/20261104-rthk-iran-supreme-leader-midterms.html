伊朗最高領袖：美中期選舉結果不會改變美方敵意

Iran's supreme leader: US midterm results will not change Washington's hostility

伊朗最高领袖：美中期选举结果不会改变美方敌意

Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba has responded to the US midterm election results, saying the Republicans' losses will not change America's hostility towards Iran, and stressing that yielding even slightly to hegemony will only embolden the other side. Iranian Parliament Speaker Ghalibaf, Tehran's chief negotiator with Washington, said the results show the American people are saying no to war. US President Donald Trump earlier attributed the Republican losses partly to the US-Israel-Iran war, but said he does not regret launching the Iran campaign and does not see the results as a personal failure.
