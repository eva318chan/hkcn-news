美國中期選舉　美媒預測民主黨將奪回眾議院多數席位

US midterms: US media project Democrats to retake House majority

美国中期选举　美媒预测民主党将夺回众议院多数席位

Voting in the US midterm elections is nearing its end, with multiple states closing polls and starting to count votes. US media project that Democrats will retake several House seats from Republicans and regain control of the House of Representatives. Early results show that in North Carolina, Democratic former governor Cooper defeated the Trump-backed Republican candidate Whatley to flip a previously Republican-held Senate seat, while incumbent Democratic Senator Ossoff won re-election in Georgia. President Trump said in an interview earlier that if Democrats win control of Congress, he may use the presidential veto to force Democrats into bipartisan deals.
