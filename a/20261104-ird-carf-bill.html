立法會通過稅務修例　加密資產申報框架明年實施、2028年起交換資料

LegCo passes crypto-asset reporting bill; automatic tax info exchange from 2028

立法会通过税务条例　加密资产申报框架明年实施、2028年起交换资料

The Legislative Council today passed the Inland Revenue (Amendment) Bill 2026 to implement the OECD's Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework and the amended Common Reporting Standard in Hong Kong. Relevant service providers must collect crypto-asset transaction information from next year for automatic exchange with partner jurisdictions starting 2028, while the amended CRS takes effect in 2028 with exchanges from 2029. Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury Christopher Hui said aligning with international standards will reinforce confidence in Hong Kong as an international financial centre.
