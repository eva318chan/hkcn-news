李家超轉抵上海　明日出席進博會開幕式及虹橋論壇

John Lee arrives in Shanghai for CIIE opening ceremony and Hongqiao Forum

李家超转抵上海　明日出席进博会开幕式及虹桥论坛

Chief Executive John Lee has arrived in Shanghai after concluding his visit to Beijing, ahead of the 9th China International Import Expo. He will tomorrow attend the opening ceremony of the CIIE and the Hongqiao International Economic Forum, as well as the forum's Hong Kong sub-forum, to share with political and business leaders from home and abroad Hong Kong's achievements and opportunities in integrating into and serving national development, and will tour the business exhibition and meet Hong Kong exhibitors. Lee said this year's CIIE draws 162 countries, regions and international organisations with exhibition space exceeding 440,000 square metres, a record high, and expressed hope to establish a 'sister port' relationship with Shanghai within this year.
