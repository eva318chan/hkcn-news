李家超北京出席京港洽談會開幕式　稱推進金融雙向開放

John Lee attends Beijing-Hong Kong economic fair opening, vows to advance two-way financial opening

李家超北京出席京港洽谈会开幕式　称推进金融双向开放

Chief Executive John Lee attended the opening ceremony of the 29th Beijing-Hong Kong Economic Cooperation Symposium and delivered an address, saying Hong Kong will press ahead with two-way financial opening. This year's fair, themed "Setting Sail for the 15th Five-Year Plan, Beijing and Hong Kong Together for the Future", aims to deepen practical cooperation between the two cities. Lee will travel to Shanghai today and attend the opening of the ninth China International Import Expo on Thursday.
