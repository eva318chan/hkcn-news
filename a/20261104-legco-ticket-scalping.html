議員關注演唱會「黃牛飛」　羅淑佩：借鏡內地做法　研究打擊炒賣

LegCo raises concert ticket scalping concerns; bureau studying countermeasures with mainland reference

议员关注演唱会「黄牛飞」　罗淑佩：借镜内地做法　研究打击炒卖

Secretary for Culture, Sports and Tourism Rosanna Law said in a written reply that the government has been concerned about ticket scalping and fake tickets, and is studying how to tackle the problem with reference to practices on the Chinese Mainland and elsewhere. She noted the existing Places of Public Entertainment Ordinance prohibits reselling tickets above face value, with offenders liable to a HK$2,000 fine on summary conviction, while the Leisure and Cultural Services Department deploys extra staff to patrol venue areas during performances. The question, raised by Reverend Canon Peter Douglas Koon, cited reports that fans could not buy tickets for a South Korean boy group's November concert through official channels, with scalpers marking prices up to three times the original.
