人行下調股票回購增持再貸款利率25基點至1厘　維穩資本市場

PBOC cuts relending rate for share buybacks and top-ups by 25 bps to 1% to stabilise capital markets

人行下调股票回购增持再贷款利率25基点至1厘　维稳资本市场

The People's Bank of China announced a series of measures to step up the relending facility for share buybacks and top-ups, aiming to keep capital markets stable. Effective today, the relending rate is cut by 25 basis points to 1 per cent. The facility covers 21 national banks and five systemically important city commercial banks, with 100 per cent support and a one-year tenor. The PBOC said banks may add a reasonable spread on top of the relending rate, and must speed up lending to boost the market-value management capacity of listed companies and major shareholders. Banks have already submitted relending applications exceeding RMB200 billion after Monday's meeting, and the PBOC will continue to fully meet banks' demand.
