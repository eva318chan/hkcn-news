《第四幕》昨晚圓方盛大首映　爾冬陞監製馮德倫主演　11月12日上映

"Afterpiece" holds star-studded premiere at Elements; produced by Derek Yee and starring Stephen Fung, opening November 12

《第四幕》昨晚圆方盛大首映　尔冬陞监制冯德伦主演　11月12日上映

"Afterpiece", the key title under the "Film Production Financing Scheme: Directors' Succession Scheme" produced by Derek Yee and directed by newcomer Wong Tak-kin, held its star-studded premiere last night (November 3) at Elements, with stars Stephen Fung, Angela Yuen, Myolie Wu and Chrissie Chau in attendance. The film opens across Hong Kong on November 12. It follows has-been director Owen (Fung), who writes, directs and stars in a new production, gradually losing himself between rehearsals and reality, culminating in an uncontrollable fiasco on opening night. Assistant Commissioner for Cultural and Creative Industries cum Secretary of the Film Development Council Maxwell Mak officiated at the ceremony, and the cast surprised birthday girl Myolie Wu with a cake near the end of the evening.
