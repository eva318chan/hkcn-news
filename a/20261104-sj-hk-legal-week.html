林定國香港法律周致辭　讚聯合國國際貿易法委員會推動貿易法律協調

Paul Lam delivers welcome remarks at Hong Kong Legal Week 2026, highlights UNCITRAL's role in trade law harmonisation

林定国香港法律周致辞　赞联合国国际贸易法委员会推动贸易法律协调

The Secretary for Justice, Paul Lam, SC, today delivered welcome remarks at Hong Kong Legal Week 2026, marking the 60th anniversary of the United Nations Commission on International Trade Law (UNCITRAL). He said that as digital transformation enables near-instant trade, the Commission has continuously advanced legal certainty for global commerce through the harmonisation and modernisation of international trade law. Lam noted that the Accra Convention, formally signed in Ghana last week, establishes a uniform legal framework for negotiable cargo documents — in both paper and electronic forms — across all modes of transport, calling it a major milestone for global trade facilitation.
