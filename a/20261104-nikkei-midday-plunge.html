日股半日跌幅擴至近4%　觸及逾3個月新低

Nikkei tumbles nearly 4% at midday to three-month low as AI and chip stocks sold off

日股半日跌幅扩至近4%　触及逾3个月新低

Tokyo stocks resumed trading after the holiday and were hit by heavy selling, with the Nikkei widening its half-day loss to nearly 4 per cent. The index touched 60,799, a more than three-month low, and closed the morning session at 60,835, down 2,246 points or about 3.6 per cent. The market followed the sell-off in US tech stocks, with AI and semiconductor-related shares seeing broad selling that dragged down the index.
