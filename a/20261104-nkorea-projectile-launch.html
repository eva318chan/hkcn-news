韓聯參：北韓今晨向東海發射不明發射體　或為彈道導彈

South Korea's JCS: North Korea fires unidentified projectile toward East Sea this morning

韩联参：北韩今晨向东海发射不明发射体　或为弹道导弹

South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff said North Korea fired an unidentified projectile toward the East Sea this morning, with South Korean and US intelligence agencies analysing its specifications and range. If confirmed as a ballistic missile, it would mark a resumption of launches after four days. North Korean state media reported on November 1 that it had conducted a test on October 31 evaluating the combat applicability and power of a tactical ballistic missile's dispersion warhead. The launch coincided with a statement by Kim Yo-jong, a Workers' Party Central Committee department director, criticising US policy toward North Korea and warning of high-intensity provocations.
