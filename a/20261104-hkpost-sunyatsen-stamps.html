「紀念孫中山先生誕辰160周年」紀念郵票及10元郵票小型張。（政府新聞處）

hk2026-11-04 14:00 · 政府新聞處

郵政署發行「紀念孫中山先生誕辰160周年」紀念郵票　11月12日公開發售

Hongkong Post to issue "Commemorating the 160th Anniversary of the Birth of Dr Sun Yat-sen" stamps, on sale November 12

邮政署发行「纪念孙中山先生诞辰160周年」纪念邮票　11月12日公开发售

Hongkong Post announced today that it will issue a set of commemorative stamps and associated philatelic products themed "Commemorating the 160th Anniversary of the Birth of Dr Sun Yat-sen", on sale from Thursday, November 12. The designs centre on Dr Sun's ties with Hong Kong, depicting his studies, revolutionary activities and public speaking in the city. The issue comprises four stamps and a HK$10 stamp sheetlet, with official first day covers available from tomorrow (November 5) at post offices and the ShopThruPost online platform.
