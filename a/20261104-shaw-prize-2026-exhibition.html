科學館辦「邵逸夫獎2026」展覽　介紹本年度得主成就　免費入場至明年1月

Hong Kong Science Museum stages "The Shaw Prize 2026 Exhibition" showcasing laureates' achievements, free admission until January 2027

科学馆办「邵逸夫奖2026」展览　介绍本年度得主成就　免费入场至明年1月

The Hong Kong Science Museum is staging "The Shaw Prize 2026 Exhibition" in its main lobby on 1/F from now until January 6, 2027, introducing this year's Shaw Laureates and their outstanding scientific contributions through multimedia and interactive panels. Admission is free. The exhibition is jointly presented by the Leisure and Cultural Services Department and The Shaw Prize Foundation. This year's Prize in Life Science and Medicine is shared by three scholars for discovering the molecular and cellular bases of acute promyelocytic leukemia and pioneering synergistic targeted therapy; the Astronomy prize goes to two scholars for studies of stellar explosions and the origin of the elements; and the Mathematical Sciences prize is shared by two scholars for breakthrough contributions to applying mathematical analysis to applied problems. A new prize category in Computer Science will be awarded for the first time in 2027.
