立法會質詢數字資產政策　陳浩濂：年底前提交修例草案　虛擬資產監管擴至交易託管

LegCo briefed on digital asset policy progress; bill to extend VA regulation by year-end

立法会质询数字资产政策　陈浩濂：年底前提交修例草案　虚拟资产监管扩至交易托管

Acting Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury Joseph Chan told the Legislative Council the government will introduce a bill by the end of the year to amend the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance, extending the existing regulatory regime for virtual asset trading platforms to dealing, advisory, management and custody services. He said the government issued its fourth batch of tokenised government bonds, worth about HK$20 billion, in September, and is advancing a legislative review to promote wider use of distributed ledger technology in the fixed income market. The reply addressed the LEAP framework under the Policy Statement 2.0 on the Development of Digital Assets, covering regulatory streamlining, tokenised products, use cases and talent development.
