議員促推廣「平安三寶」　孫玉菡：無迫切需要修例擴持久授權書涵蓋個人照顧

LegCo urges promotion of "three instruments of peace"; no pressing need to amend EPA law

议员促推广「平安三宝」　孙玉菡：无迫切需要修例扩持久授权书涵盖个人照顾

Secretary for Labour and Welfare Chris Sun told the Legislative Council in a written reply that the government sees no pressing need to amend the law to extend enduring powers of attorney to cover a donor's personal care, since arrangements covering such matters generally already involve the use of the donor's income or capital. He set out the legal bases of the "three instruments of peace" - wills under the Wills Ordinance, enduring powers of attorney under the Enduring Powers of Attorney Ordinance, and advance medical directives under the Advance Decision on Life-sustaining Treatment Ordinance. The reply also addressed proposals for an official voluntary registration system for the three instruments and cross-boundary recognition within the Greater Bay Area.
