陳茂波鑑賞家盛會晚宴致辭　香港成全球最大跨境財富管理中心

Paul Chan touts Hong Kong as world's largest cross-boundary wealth management centre at Festival of Connoisseurs gala

陈茂波鉴赏家盛会晚宴致辞　香港成全球最大跨境财富管理中心

Financial Secretary Paul Chan said Hong Kong has become the world's largest cross-boundary wealth management centre, with more than 18,000 ultra-high-net-worth individuals and over 3,300 single family offices. Speaking at the Festival of Connoisseurs Gala Dinner, Chan said the inaugural festival — featuring nine premium clusters, more than 50 world-renowned brands and over 3,000 overseas visitors — is the first exhibition supported under the Government's $100 million initiative to attract large-scale international exhibitions. He also highlighted Project Skytopia, under which the Airport Authority will open a museum-grade art storage facility in early 2027.
