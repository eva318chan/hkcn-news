2026 Opportunities for the Elderly Project awards honour active ageing as centenarians top 10,000

2026「老有所为活动计划」颁奖　孙玉菡：全港百岁人瑞逾万人

The Social Welfare Department held the 2026 Opportunities for the Elderly Project award ceremony today, presenting 11 special awards, 22 district best awards and six Hong Kong best awards to commend outstanding organisations and elderly volunteers. Secretary for Labour and Welfare Chris Sun said Hong Kong's centenarians have exceeded 10,000, and the Government extends birthday greetings to them every year as a sign of respect. Since 1998 the project has subsidised nearly 8,000 programmes with over 4.7 million participants; tomorrow marks OEP Day, with elderly ambassadors leading exercise sessions at elderly centres citywide.
