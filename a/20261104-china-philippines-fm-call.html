中菲外長電話會談　就南海問題同意維持對話

China and Philippines foreign ministers hold phone talks, agree to maintain dialogue on South China Sea

中菲外长电话会谈　就南海问题同意维持对话

According to NHK, with South China Sea sovereignty disputes continuing, the foreign ministers of China and the Philippines held phone talks yesterday and agreed to maintain dialogue. Both countries are set to attend international meetings in the middle of this month, and the talks sought to stabilise bilateral relations ahead of those events. Friction between China and the Philippines over the South China Sea has deepened in recent years, and the call is seen as a signal of easing tensions.
