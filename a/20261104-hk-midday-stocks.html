HK midday review: Hang Seng flat; tech stocks fall, catering and consumer stocks active

港股午评：恒指基本平收　科网股普跌餐饮消费股活跃

Hong Kong's three major indices dipped slightly in the morning session before recovering, with the Hang Seng Index and the Hang Seng China Enterprises Index closing the morning session roughly flat, while the Hang Seng Tech Index fell about 1 per cent. Large-cap tech stocks that had rebounded for several sessions broadly retreated, with Alibaba and JD.com down about 2 per cent and Tencent, Xiaomi and Meituan slightly lower. Catering and consumer stocks mostly stayed active, while biotech, shipping and oil stocks largely rose and auto, property and optical communication stocks remained subdued.
