署理創科局局長：本港初創逾5,200間　加強政府投資帶動私人資本

Acting innovation chief: over 5,200 start-ups in Hong Kong; government investment to mobilise private capital

署理创科局局长：本港初创逾5,200间　加强政府投资带动私人资本

The Acting Secretary for Innovation, Technology and Industry told the Cyberport Venture & Capital Forum 2026 that Hong Kong's start-up ecosystem now counts more than 5,200 start-ups, up 11 per cent from last year, with Hong Kong Science Park and Cyberport together nurturing about 20 unicorns. She said the enhanced Innovation and Technology Venture Fund will see government provision rise to HK$2.5 billion, expected to leverage at least HK$7.5 billion in market capital, while the new HK$10 billion industry-oriented fund is expected to reach at least HK$40 billion including market contributions. This year's forum, themed "Scaling in a Changing World: Winning the Next Competitive Cycle", explores how enterprises can scale across the Greater Bay Area, the Middle East, ASEAN and Central Asia.
