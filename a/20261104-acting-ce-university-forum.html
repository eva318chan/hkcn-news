Acting CE: Hong Kong the world's only city with five top-100 universities; building university towns in Northern Metropolis

陈国基：香港全球唯一拥有五间百强大学城市　建北部都会区大学城

Acting Chief Executive Chan Kwok-ki delivered a speech at the opening of the World Class University Presidents Forum 2026 at HKUST today. He noted Hong Kong is the only city in the world with five universities in the global top 100, and the QS Asia University Rankings released yesterday placed five Hong Kong universities in Asia's top 10. The Government published Hong Kong's first five-year plan in September to accelerate the integrated development of education, technology and talent, he said. Three university towns will be built in the Northern Metropolis alongside the Hetao Shenzhen-Hong Kong Science and Technology Innovation Cooperation Zone, serving as Hong Kong's launchpad into the Greater Bay Area, while the Study in Hong Kong initiative continues to attract top global scholars and students.
