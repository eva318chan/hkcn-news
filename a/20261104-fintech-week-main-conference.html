香港金融科技周主論壇圓滿結束　聚焦金融＋、AI＋及全球聯繫

Hong Kong FinTech Week x StartmeupHK 2026 main conference concludes, focusing on Finance+, AI+ and global connectivity

香港金融科技周主论坛圆满结束　聚焦金融＋、AI＋及全球联系

The two-day main conference of Hong Kong FinTech Week x StartmeupHK 2026 concluded yesterday (November 3) at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre, attracting more than 30 delegations from the Chinese Mainland and international markets to explore new opportunities under the three core growth engines of Finance+, AI+ and Global Connectivity. Chief Executive John Lee delivered opening remarks, noting that Hong Kong's first Five-Year Plan, unveiled less than two months ago, provides a clear direction for businesses, investors and innovators and is aligned with the National 15th Five-Year Plan. Financial Secretary Paul Chan set out three priorities for advancing fintech: efficiency as the engine, inclusion as the purpose, and trust as the foundation. The week-long programme continues through November 6 with community and partner events, plus a two-day GBA Immersive Executive Tour to Shenzhen.
