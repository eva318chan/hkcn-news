Cheung Kwok-kwan closes Hong Kong Legal Week 2026, pledges continued contribution to UNCITRAL's digital-economy trade work

香港法律周2026闭幕　张国钧：香港续为贸易法委员会数码经济货运工作出力

Deputy Secretary for Justice Cheung Kwok-kwan delivered closing remarks at "Hong Kong Legal Week 2026: UNCITRAL's Contribution to the International Transport of Goods in the Digital Economy – A Celebration of UNCITRAL@60". Cheung said UNCITRAL has excellent collaboration with the Department of Justice, and Hong Kong has made valuable contributions to its work on the international transport of goods in the digital economy in the past and will continue to do so. The day's four panels covered the digitalisation and platformisation of trade finance, digital transferable records, smart contracts and cross-border dispute resolution, with constructive dialogue among members of the Judiciary, policymakers, legal scholars, practitioners and stakeholders.
