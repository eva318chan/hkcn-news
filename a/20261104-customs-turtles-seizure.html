Customs seizes five suspected endangered live turtles disguised as toy shipment at airport

海关机场截查报称积木玩具邮包　检5只怀疑濒危活龟

Hong Kong Customs seized five live turtles of suspected scheduled endangered species, worth about $5,000, at Hong Kong International Airport today. Officers intercepted a parcel from the United States declared as building block toys and found the live turtles inside upon inspection. The case has been handed to the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department; importing endangered species without a licence carries a maximum penalty of a $10 million fine and 10 years' imprisonment.
