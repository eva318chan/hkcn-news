騰訊發布開源AI模型Hy4　過濾網絡安全數據防濫用

Tencent launches open-source AI model Hy4 with cybersecurity data filtered out

腾讯发布开源AI模型Hy4　过滤网络安全数据防滥用

Tencent today launched its new open-source AI model Hy4, whose training filtered out cybersecurity-related data such as vulnerability exploitation, reverse engineering and penetration testing, while adding 'defensive' content to reduce the risk of the model being used for cyberattacks. The company said it recognises the potential security risks posed by advanced cyber and biological capabilities, and pledged a more responsible approach across training, open-sourcing and release. The move comes amid a surge in AI-driven cyberattacks globally, raising industry concerns about the potential threats of advanced models.
