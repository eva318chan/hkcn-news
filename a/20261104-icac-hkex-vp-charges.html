廉署起訴港交所前副總裁湯寶衡　涉不誠實取用內部資訊系統　母同被控

ICAC charges former HKEX vice president Geoffrey Tong over alleged dishonest access to internal system; mother also charged

廉署起诉港交所前副总裁汤宝衡　涉不诚实取用内部资讯系统　母同被控

The ICAC has charged former HKEX vice president Geoffrey Tong, 39, and his mother Leung Yiu-chun, 68, jointly with one count of obtaining access to a computer with a view to dishonest gain. The charge alleges the pair dishonestly accessed HKEX's internal information system, which contained confidential information of listed companies, between February 2018 and April 2023. Tong allegedly accessed confidential information of listed companies unrelated to his duties on multiple occasions, and his mother's subsequent share trades earned over HK$500,000. The case was mentioned at Eastern Magistrates' Courts today; the ICAC said the investigation continues and further prosecutions are not ruled out. The defendants are presumed innocent unless proven guilty.
