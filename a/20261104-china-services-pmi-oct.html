內地10月服務業PMI跌至50.8　擴張速度3個月最慢

China October services PMI slips to 50.8, slowest expansion in three months

内地10月服务业PMI跌至50.8　扩张速度3个月最慢

China's October services PMI compiled by S&P Global fell to 50.8 from 51.6 in September, the slowest pace of expansion in three months amid capacity constraints. New business remained in relatively quick expansion, though growth in both new orders and new export orders slowed slightly. Firms added full-time and part-time staff to handle workloads, and backlogs of work fell for the first time in about a year. Amid intensifying competition, services firms cut selling prices for a second straight month, while input prices rose at the fastest pace in five months. The October composite PMI slipped to 52 from 52.4, with business activity growth also the slowest in three months.
