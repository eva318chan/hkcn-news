陳茂波為首屆「Festival of Connoisseurs」開幕　展超級遊艇跑車　倡發展遊艇經濟

Paul Chan opens inaugural Festival of Connoisseurs, highlights yacht economy potential

陈茂波为首届「Festival of Connoisseurs」开幕　展超级游艇跑车　倡发展游艇经济

Financial Secretary Paul Chan officiated at the grand opening of the inaugural Festival of Connoisseurs at AsiaWorld-Expo, an exhibition showcasing superyachts, supercars, private jets, fine jewellery and exceptional wines. He said Hong Kong's convention and exhibition industry contributed more than HK$50 billion to the economy, and that the festival is the first event under the Budget's HK$100 million initiative to attract high-quality international exhibitions. Chan also highlighted the potential of Hong Kong's yacht economy, noting the Airport Authority's Skytopia project plans to provide 500 yacht berths, with northbound yacht travel having begun in June and the first batch of southbound yachts from Guangdong arriving under a new scheme.
