海關舉行學員結業會操　檢閱9名見習督察及35名見習關員

Customs holds passing-out parade for 9 probationary inspectors and 35 officers

海关举行学员结业会操　检阅9名见习督察及35名见习关员

Hong Kong Customs today held a passing-out parade at the Hong Kong Customs College for the 147th Inspector Induction Course and the 511th to 512th Customs Officer Induction Courses, with 9 probationary inspectors and 35 probationary customs officers reviewed by Mr Tang Yat-shun. The parade featured a Chinese-style foot drill performance. In his address, Mr Tang said Hong Kong Customs is a key force in implementing Hong Kong's first five-year plan and aligning with national strategies, urging graduates to stay loyal and serve the people through law enforcement. He added that under the national 15th Five-Year Plan, the smart upgrade of the Huanggang port and hard-and-soft connectivity of Greater Bay Area ports bring new missions for Customs.
