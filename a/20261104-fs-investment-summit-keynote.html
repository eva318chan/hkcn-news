Paul Chan: Hong Kong broadens financing choices, with HK$388b IPO proceeds in first nine months

陈茂波：香港拓宽融资与配置选择　今年首三季IPO集资额3,880亿元

Financial Secretary Paul Chan delivered the keynote speech at the Global Financial Leaders' Investment Summit - Conversations with Global Investors today. He said businesses at different development stages need different forms of capital, and Hong Kong's role is to bring businesses and investors together. Hong Kong's venture capital and private equity ecosystem manages around US$250 billion in assets, second in Asia, while the Hong Kong Investment Corporation has catalysed long-term capital into early and growth-stage companies. In the first nine months of this year, IPO proceeds in Hong Kong reached HK$388 billion and follow-on fundraising HK$570 billion. Chan also highlighted progress on the fixed income, currency and commodity market, with dim sum bond outstanding stock close to RMB1.9 trillion, and stressed that trust is the greatest asset of an international financial centre.
