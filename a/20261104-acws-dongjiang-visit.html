Water supplies advisory body begins Guangdong visit to Dongjiang supply system

供水谘委会访粤考察东江供水系统　西江调水工程可作本港紧急备用水源

A delegation of the Advisory Committee on Water Supplies began a two-day visit to Guangdong today to inspect the Dongjiang Water Supply System. The delegation visited the Gaoxinsha Reservoir in Nansha to learn about the Pearl River Delta Water Resources Allocation Project, commissioned in 2024, which diverts Xijiang River water to the eastern Pearl River Delta and can serve as an emergency backup water source for Hong Kong. Members also observed Dongjiang water quality at a Huizhou greenway and the basin's intelligent monitoring centre, and will inspect the Dongguan intake point tomorrow.
