國台辦回應台媒　指大陸海警在台島以東常態化執法巡查

Taiwan Affairs Office responds to Taiwan media: mainland coast guard conducting regular patrols east of Taiwan island

国台办回应台媒　指大陆海警在台岛以东常态化执法巡查

The Taiwan Affairs Office of the State Council held its regular press conference this morning, where spokesperson Zhu Fenglian responded to questions from Taiwan media. A Taiwan reporter cited US media reports that mainland vessels have been sailing back and forth across waters south and east of Taiwan, asking whether the activities go beyond law enforcement toward military purposes. Zhu said the mainland coast guard conducts regular law-enforcement patrols in waters east of Taiwan island, continuously strengthening control over the relevant waters to safeguard normal navigation and operations, calling it a just and necessary move to defend national territorial sovereignty and maritime rights.
