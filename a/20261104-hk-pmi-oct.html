香港10月PMI升至50.2　三個月來首次重返擴張水平

Hong Kong October PMI rises to 50.2, back in expansion for first time in three months

香港10月PMI升至50.2　三个月来首次重返扩张水平

Hong Kong's October PMI compiled by S&P Global rose to 50.2 from 49.2 in September, returning to expansion territory for the first time in three months. The survey showed new orders resumed expansion after two months of decline, new export orders rose for a second straight month, and new orders from the Mainland posted their biggest increase in eight months. But the survey also flashed a cautious signal: input costs rose sharply with purchase prices hitting a five-month high, and firms' confidence in the one-year business outlook fell to its lowest in more than six years amid concerns over the economic environment and the global situation.
