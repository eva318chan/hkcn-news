警方搗洗黑錢集團拘11人　涉85宗騙案2,400萬元

Police bust money-laundering syndicate, arrest 11 in connection with 85 fraud cases worth HK$24 million

警方捣洗黑钱集团拘11人　涉85宗骗案2,400万元

Police launched a two-day enforcement operation against a money-laundering syndicate, arresting 11 people – eight men and three women – on suspicion of conspiracy to defraud, money laundering and criminal intimidation. The operation was connected to 85 fraud cases involving about HK$24 million. Police reminded the public not to lend bank accounts to others to avoid becoming money-laundering tools.
