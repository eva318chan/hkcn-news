花旗調查：本港92%消費者用AI　逾半憂跟不上技術發展

Citi survey: 92% of Hong Kong consumers use AI, but over half worry about falling behind

花旗调查：本港92%消费者用AI　逾半忧跟不上技术发展

Citi Hong Kong today released the 2026 Citi Wealth AI Pulse survey, finding AI usage among Hong Kong consumers as high as 92%, yet more than half (52%) of respondents worry they cannot keep up with AI development. The survey, conducted online in early September among 1,053 local consumers aged 21 to 69, found the top three AI uses in shopping are comparing prices, brands or merchants; looking up product information and reviews; and discovering new products or brands, with about 53% of respondents planning to try agentic commerce within 12 months. In wealth management, however, consumers still trust human professional judgement: among emerging affluent respondents, 77% prefer AI to provide information or advice, but only 4% are willing to hand investment management fully to AI.
