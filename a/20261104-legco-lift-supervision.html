議員促加強升降機工程監管　發展局：新裝升降機須經三級檢驗始發准用證

LegCo presses for stronger supervision of lift works; three-tier inspection required for new lifts

议员促加强升降机工程监管　发展局：新装升降机须经三级检验始发准用证

The Development Bureau told the Legislative Council that every newly installed lift must pass a three-tier inspection - self-inspection by the registered contractor, thorough examination by a registered engineer, and on-site re-inspection and functional testing of all safety components by the Electrical and Mechanical Services Department - before a use permit is issued. The question, raised by lawmaker Holden Chow, cited recently uncovered deficiencies in lift works at housing estates, including lifts installed without fire-resisting doors and door frame components to specification, as well as frequent breakdowns of new lifts at Queen Mary Hospital's new Clinical Block due to shaft deviations and installation problems.
