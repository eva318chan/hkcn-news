孫東出席烏鎮互聯網大會峰會　明年與AI院合推AI智能體安全治理指引

Sun Dong attends WIC Wuzhen Summit, pledges AI Agent safety guidelines with HK AI R&D Institute next year

孙东出席乌镇互联网大会峰会　明年与AI院合推AI智能体安全治理指引

Secretary for Innovation, Technology and Industry Sun Dong attended the 2026 World Internet Conference Wuzhen Summit, joining the Global Cooperation and Innovation Forum on mutual trust, interconnectivity and interoperability of AI Agents as well as the Cross-Strait, Hong Kong and Macao Internet Development Forum, plus the opening ceremony and ministerial meeting where he exchanged views on Hong Kong's I&T development with governments, international organisations, enterprises and research institutes. Sun said the government is advancing AI and AI Agents in three areas: promoting wide application, improving the governance framework, and fostering international exchanges and co-operation. He said the government and the Hong Kong Artificial Intelligence Research and Development Institute will jointly issue AI Agent safety-management guidelines applicable to the entire community next year, and Hong Kong will host a large-scale international AI conference to support the country's Global AI Governance Initiative, promoting open source, openness, co-operation and sharing.
