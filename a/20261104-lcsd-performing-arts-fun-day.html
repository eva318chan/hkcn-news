康文署辦表演藝術同樂日2026　屯門荃灣兩大會堂免費入場

LCSD to stage Performing Arts Fun Day 2026 at Tuen Mun and Tsuen Wan town halls, free admission

康文署办表演艺术同乐日2026　屯门荃湾两大会堂免费入场

The Leisure and Cultural Services Department will stage "Performing Arts Fun Day 2026" at Tuen Mun Town Hall and Tsuen Wan Town Hall in November, presenting free performances of music, dance and theatre, plus workshops and exhibitions. The Tuen Mun event starts at 2pm on Sunday, November 8, featuring dance and Chinese music performances, Fujian Nanyin music and storytelling workshops. The Tsuen Wan event starts at 2pm on Sunday, November 15, with the Music Office's youth orchestras staging symphony and string concerts, plus Chinese-Western crossover concerts and Cantonese music theatre. Some events with limited places require pre-registration or admission tickets, on a first-come, first-served basis.
