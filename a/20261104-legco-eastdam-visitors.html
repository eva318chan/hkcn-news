議員關注東壩遊客分流　環境局：檢討破邊洲預約制　研長遠郊野管理策略

LegCo questions East Dam visitor management; government to review Po Pin Chau booking trial

议员关注东坝游客分流　环境局：检讨破边洲预约制　研长远郊野管理策略

Secretary for Environment and Ecology Tse Chin-wan told the Legislative Council the government will review the trial online reservation system for the Po Pin Chau section of the High Island Geo Trail at Sai Kung's East Dam and formulate a long-term country park management strategy. An interdepartmental working group chaired by the Chief Secretary for Administration co-ordinated visitor flow, transport support and facility capacity during the Golden Week period, while the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department rolled out targeted measures on management, nature conservation and visitor education at the East Dam. The question also asked about the government's 2019 plan to develop Pak Tam Chung into a green tourism hub.
