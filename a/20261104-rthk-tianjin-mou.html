廣播處長關婉儀訪京津　與天津海河傳媒集團簽合作備忘錄

Broadcasting chief Angelina Kwan signs MOU with Tianjin Haihe Media Group

广播处长关婉仪访京津　与天津海河传媒集团签合作备忘录

Director of Broadcasting Angelina Kwan returned to Hong Kong after a four-day visit to the Chinese Mainland, during which she signed a memorandum of understanding with Tianjin Haihe Media Group president Ma Bo. The MOU covers programme resources, talent exchanges, platform and copyright licensing co-operation, and on-ground events. Haihe Media operates across newspapers, radio, television, websites and new media in Tianjin; the partnership will help distribute RTHK content to the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region and bring Haihe's productions to international audiences through RTHK's platforms. Kwan also called on the Hong Kong and Macao Affairs Office of the State Council, the National Radio and Television Administration and Beijing Radio and Television Station, and said RTHK has signed MOUs with eight mainland and overseas media organisations this year.
